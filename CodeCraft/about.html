About CodeCraft

A beginner-friendly learning resource that makes web development feel approachable, structured and genuinely enjoyable.

Who we are

What Is CodeCraft?

CodeCraft is an educational website for people who have never written a line of code. It brings together clear tutorials, a step-by-step roadmap, hands-on practice projects and a curated list of trustworthy tools.

The focus is on the foundations: how websites work, and how to build them with HTML and CSS. Strong fundamentals make everything that comes later, including JavaScript and modern frameworks, much easier to learn.

Our Mission

To help beginners understand web development step by step, so that anyone with curiosity and a computer can build and publish a website of their own.

✦

Our Vision

A world where learning to code feels welcoming rather than intimidating, and where good habits like accessibility and clean structure are taught from day one.

Why it matters

Why Beginners Should Learn Web Development

  • Immediate feedback. Save a file, refresh the browser and see the result.
  • Low barrier to entry. You need only a text editor and a browser, both free.
  • Creative and practical. You can build something personal or useful right away.
  • A transferable foundation. HTML and CSS underpin every web technology you will meet later.
  • Many career paths. Web skills apply in design, development, content, marketing and more.
