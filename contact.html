Get in Touch

CodeCraft is an educational project and does not publish a mailing address, phone number or support inbox. Because there is no live contact channel on this site, the demonstration form cannot deliver messages.

We welcome your feedback

If this site is hosted by you or your school, add your own form service or contact details to the form’s action attribute to make it work. Suggestions for new lessons, corrections and ideas for projects are always valuable.

Frequently Asked Support Questions

A page or link looks broken. What can I do?

Refresh the page, check that you opened the file from inside the CodeCraft folder, and keep the css and js folders next to the HTML files.

Can I use CodeCraft offline?

Yes. Open index.html in your browser. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so without a connection your browser will use fallback fonts instead.

Where can I find more help learning?

See the Resources page and the FAQ.
